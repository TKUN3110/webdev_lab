System Architecture & Workflow

Forecast and Early Warning Workflow

  1. Historical Meteorological Data Acquisition from IMD Repositories
  2. Data Quality Control, Cleaning, and Spatio-Temporal Pre-processing
  3. AI Model Development for Region-wise Temperature Forecasting
  4. Localized Real-Time Ground Observation via IoT-enabled AWS Infrastructure
  5. Forecast Validation and Regional Hotspot Identification
  6. LLM-Assisted Automated Stakeholder Advisory Generation
  7. Multi-channel Advisory Dissemination to Authorities and Public

Primary Data Sources

  • AWS Sensors: IoT-enabled Automated Weather Stations measuring localized ambient temperature and humidity.
  • IMD Database: Long-term historical grid temperature observations across 7 climatic zones in India.
  • Satellite Feeds: Remote sensing thermal imagery for land surface temperature (LST) mapping.

Stakeholder Roles & Available Actions

  • State Disaster Management Authorities (SDMA)
    • Issue regional heat emergency alerts and deploy cooling centers.
    • Coordinate emergency water distribution and relief operations.
  • Municipal Corporations & Health Departments
    • Monitor hospital heat-stroke admission rates.
    • Enforce mandatory rest schedules for outdoor municipal workers.
  • Agricultural Agencies & Farmers
    • Access crop heat stress advisory reports.
    • Adjust irrigation timing to early morning and evening hours.
  • General Citizens
    • Subscribe to real-time SMS and WhatsApp heat advisories.
    • View interactive regional heatwave hotspot maps.

Technical Terms & Abbreviations Explained

AWS
Automated Weather Station — IoT-enabled ground sensor units deployed for continuous localized weather observations.
IMD
India Meteorological Department — National meteorological agency providing historical datasets and official weather forecasts.
HW
Heatwave — Condition where maximum temperature departure from normal is 4.5°C to 6.4°C.
SHW
Severe Heatwave — Extreme weather condition where maximum temperature departure from normal exceeds 6.4°C or absolute temp exceeds 47°C.
