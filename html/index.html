Welcome to the Heatwave Intelligence Web Portal

Our Mission

Providing AI-enabled climate intelligence solutions for continuous heatwave monitoring, temperature forecasting, hotspot identification, and early warning dissemination across India.

Developed under Use Case in collaboration with the India Meteorological Department (IMD), this portal integrates historical meteorological datasets with real-time ground observations from IoT-enabled Automated Weather Stations (AWS).
