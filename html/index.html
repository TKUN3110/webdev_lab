Welcome to the Heatwave Intelligence Web Portal

Our Mission

Providing AI-enabled climate intelligence solutions for continuous heatwave monitoring, temperature forecasting, hotspot identification, and early warning dissemination across India.

Developed in collaboration with the India Meteorological Department (IMD), this portal integrates historical meteorological datasets with real-time ground observations from IoT-enabled Automated Weather Stations (AWS).

🔥 Live Alerts

Real-time heatwave detection and severe weather warnings for high-risk zones.

📊 Temperature Forecast

7-day AI forecast powered by IoT ground sensors and satellite imagery.

🛡️ Public Advisory

Actionable medical guidelines and agricultural irrigation protection tips.
