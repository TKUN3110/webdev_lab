Bulletin Version: v2.1 | Date: 9th August 2026 | Issued By: IMD Climate Alert Division

⚠️ SEVERE HEATWAVE ALERT: North Konkan, Vidarbha & Marathwada

The IMD along with IoT-enabled AWS units has issued a severe warning. Maximum ambient temperature recorded is 47.2°C with real-feel heat index reaching 53.5°C.

Region Zone Observed Max Temp Departure from Normal Alert Severity Level
Vidarbha (Chandrapur) 47.2°C +6.8°C SHW Level 4
Central Zone (Solapur) 46.8°C +5.9°C SHW Level 4
North Zone (Jalgaon) 43.5°C +3.8°C HW Level 2

🛡️ Public Safety Guidelines & Advisories

  • Hydration Advisory: Drink plenty of water and carry oral rehydration salts (ORS) when traveling.
  • Work Safety Advisory: Avoid continuous outdoor physical labor between 11:00 AM and 4:00 PM.
  • Health Advisory: Keep children and elderly persons in shaded or ventilated indoor areas.
